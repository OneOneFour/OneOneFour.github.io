    Icons

    Phosphor duotone icons with a 2px outline in the text colour and a flat fill from the palette. Brand logos stay one flat colour, and the colour goes on the button around them.

    Dark mode keeps the colour bright. The page goes to a deep night blue, the fills stay vivid, and labels on fills stay dark. Outlines and shadows go near-black, so the toon edges still read as depth.

    Tints turn dark. Sky, Lilac and Apricot are panel backgrounds, so in dark mode they become deep versions with light text and don't glare.

    Coral is easier in dark. In light mode it takes white text at large sizes only. In dark mode, the slightly brighter coral carries ink text at 4.7:1.
